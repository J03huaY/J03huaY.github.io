# Writeup

## What was the most challenging part of this assignment? Did you find HTML and CSS easy or difficult to work with?

The most challenging part is to have a feel of what 1px is. It is really hard to just know how far you want one thing is from another. But HTML and CSS are not that difficult to work with, even though I relied heavily on AI agents to guide me, especially with those format options. 

## How did you build the puzzle grid, and what other options did you consider?

I want one related to sports since I'm a sports fan. But it took me a while to understand how this puzzle grid works so I asked Codex to generate one that includes basketball and football. It is not a perfect one. If I had more time I would probably come up with another one. And I thought about the locations of those black cells. Finally, all the white cells are input for one letter and I created another css class for blackcells to make them just placeholders. I did not explore other layouts.

## What did you take into account when designing the site? Is there anything you are particularly proud of?

I want one that is really simple since it looks nice and I don't know what is next so it leaves space for me to add on later. There is not really anything that I'm really proud of, but I am happy about the Navbar. It looks simple and nice.

## Given more time or resources, what would you add?

Probably a fancier animation for the puzzle grid so that when users actually finish the puzzle there will be an animation for congratulations. So there will be an auto complete detection.  Also, maybe add some fire animation on the chosen grid.

## How many hours did you spend on this assignment?

About 10 hours.

## Assumptions (optional)

I just assumed I would have something simple for later add-ons.
